## When does the Walmart Black Friday sale start?

Walmart hasn't published its 2026 schedule, but expect three separate events rather than one big day. In 2025, a deals sneak peek ran November 14–16, the main Black Friday event ran November 25–30 with the first three days online-only, and Cyber Monday followed on December 1. If Walmart repeats that structure this year, the main event should run **November 24 through 29**, with Cyber Monday exclusively online on November 30. Slickdeals, which tracks these calendars closely, projects a first event around November 13–15.

Official confirmation usually lands in late October — October 27 in 2025, October 28 in 2024, October 31 in 2023 — so an announcement is likely days away as of this writing. Walmart's own deal page puts it plainly: the Black Friday sale typically runs about two weeks, with early deals kicking off in mid-November.

The store logistics have been stable too. Walmart stores closed on Thanksgiving Day, opened at **6 a.m. local time** on Black Friday itself in both 2024 and 2025, and online shopping continued through the holiday. Check the store locator close to the date, since individual locations can deviate.

One detail that shapes the whole event: in 2025, the November 25–27 portion of the Black Friday sale was **online only**, with in-store availability starting November 28. Plan around the website, and treat store shelves as the backup.
